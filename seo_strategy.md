# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and support-facing web pages served from `artifacts/chatbot/`
- Any public HTML landing page served by `artifacts/mobile/server/serve.js`

## Out of scope
- API endpoints under `/api/**`
- Native Expo app screens that are not crawlable HTML pages
- Internal development surfaces such as `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/`

## Target audience
- Travelers planning tours and excursions in Natal/RN
- Portuguese-speaking prospective customers looking for help with passeio selection, pricing, and booking

## Primary keywords
- passeios em Natal
- turismo em Natal RN
- agência de passeios em Natal
- reservas de passeios em Natal
